Halloween hazards

  • Chocolate is toxic to dogs and cats. Darker chocolate is more dangerous.
  • Sugar-free sweets and gum can contain xylitol, which is very dangerous for dogs.
  • Keep raisins, wrappers and glow sticks out of reach.
  • Doorbells and costumes stress many pets. A quiet room helps.

If your pet has eaten something it shouldn't, call (604) 888-2628 right away.
