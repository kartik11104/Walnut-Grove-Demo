# P1 Home page
Allowed files: `template.html` (only inside `#v-home` and the CSS it uses)
Goal: make the home page clearer and calmer for a worried pet owner, in under 10 seconds they should see: what we are, are we open now, how to call, how to book.
Do:
- Check the first screen on a phone: logo, one clear sentence, a Call button and a Book button, and the open-now line.
- Make the three most useful things (book, call, directions) the biggest buttons.
- Shorten any paragraph over 3 lines.
Do not: add numbers of years or cases, reviews, or awards.
Done when: tests pass; phone first screen shows Call and Book without scrolling; the Reviewer checklist passes.
