## SMALL MAMMAL MEDICINE

Small mammals such as rabbits and pocket pets require specialized medical knowledge and handling. Our veterinary team provides tailored examinations and treatment plans designed specifically for these unique companions, including specialized dental care for species with continuously growing teeth.

Because small mammals often hide signs of illness, early evaluation and preventative care are especially important in maintaining their health. Diagnostic bloodwork can reveal hidden conditions.

#### Key Small Mammal Services Include

  * Routine wellness examinations designed specifically for rabbits, guinea pigs, and other small mammals.
  * Nutritional and habitat consultations to ensure optimal living conditions and balanced diets.
  * Dental and digestive health evaluations addressing common issues in small mammals.
  * Diagnostic testing and treatment plans for illness or behavioural changes.
  * Preventative health recommendations tailored to the unique needs of small companion animals.

With attentive care and early intervention, we aim to support the long-term health and wellbeing of every small mammal we treat.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### What sorts of small mammals do you care for, really?

We handle veterinary care for rabbits, guinea pigs and other small companion mammals, with services tailored to each animal's health and wellness needs, not generic stuff.

### Why are regular wellness exams important for small mammals?

Small mammals usually hide signs of illness, making regular wellness exams important for determining potential health problems before they become more serious. 

### Do you provide help with diet and living space care?

Yes, we offer health advice and habitat consultations to support a balanced diet and a suitable living environment for your pet. 

### Can you assist with dental problems in rabbits, plus other small mammals?

Yes, we do dental evaluations and help with common dental issues, which is especially key for species whose teeth keep growing. 

### What should I do if my small mammal's behavior suddenly changes?

If behavior changes quickly, it can mean an underlying health problem. We suggest booking an examination so our unit can review your pet and decide whether diagnostic testing or treatment is required.
