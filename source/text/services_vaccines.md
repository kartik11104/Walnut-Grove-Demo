## VACCINATIONS

Vaccinations play a critical role in protecting pets from infectious and potentially life-threatening diseases. As part of our preventative care programs, our vaccination protocols are designed to provide effective protection while considering your pet's lifestyle, age, and individual risk factors.

Our team follows evidence-based guidelines to ensure each pet receives the appropriate immunizations without unnecessary treatments. Vaccines are often paired with parasite prevention for comprehensive protection.

#### Key Vaccination Services Include

  * Core vaccinations for dogs and cats to protect against highly contagious and serious diseases.
  * Lifestyle-based non-core vaccines recommended based on your pet's environment, travel, and exposure risks.
  * Structured puppy and kitten vaccination schedules to safely build immunity during early development.
  * Adult booster vaccinations to maintain long-term disease protection throughout your pet's life.
  * Personalized vaccine consultations to evaluate your pet's health status and determine the most appropriate schedule.

A strong start during puppy-hood and kitten-hood helps create the foundation for a lifetime of good health. Learn more about our puppy & kitten care programs.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### Where can I get pet vaccinations in Langley, BC?

Walnut Grove Animal Hospital provides pet vaccinations in Langley, BC for dogs and cats covering both core and lifestyle vaccines based on your pet's age and risk factors.

### Why are vaccinations important for my dog or cat?

Vaccinations help protect dogs and cats against serious, highly contagious, and potentially life-threatening infectious diseases. They provide essential support for your pet's long-term immune system health and contribute to overall community disease prevention.

### What is the puppy or kitten vaccine schedule and timeline?

Vaccines typically start at 6–8 weeks, with boosters every 3–4 weeks until the series finishes around 14–16 weeks old.

### What core vaccines does my dog or cat actually need?

Dogs need rabies, distemper, adenovirus, and parvovirus (DHPP); cats need rabies and FVRCP (herpesvirus, calicivirus, panleukopenia) recommended for nearly all pets.

### What vaccines are recommended for cat vaccination in Langley?

Cats need core vaccines rabies and FVRCP (herpesvirus, calicivirus, panleukopenia) plus feline leukemia if they go outdoors or live with other cats.

### What non-core (lifestyle) vaccines might my pet need?

Vaccines like Bordetella, leptospirosis or feline leukemia are recommended selectively based on boarding, outdoor access or exposure to other animals.

### Can vaccination plans be customized for my pet?

Yes, we provide personalized vaccine consultations. This allows us to review your pet’s individual risk factors and design a customized protection plan tailored to their specific lifestyle.

### Is rabies vaccination legally required for pets in Langley, BC? 

It is not mandated province-wide, but most boarding, grooming and travel providers require proof, ask Walnut Grove Animal Hospital about Langley Township requirements.

### How often do adult pets need vaccine boosters?

It depends on the vaccine, some core vaccines last up to 3 years, while others need annual re-vaccination.

### Can my pet have a reaction to a vaccine and what should I watch for?

Mild soreness or tiredness for 24–48 hours is common; facial swelling or vomiting is rare but needs immediate veterinary attention.

### How much does a vaccine appointment cost in BC?

Roughly $30–$60 CAD per vaccine with discounted combo packages often available for puppy/kitten series.

### What should I look for in a dog vaccine clinic near me?

Look for a clinic offering structured core vaccine protocols, puppy series scheduling and booster tracking. Walnut Grove Animal Hospital in Langley offers all three and is open 7 days a week.
