## DOG BREEDING

We provide professional veterinary support for breeders, focused on canine reproductive health and successful litters. Our services aim to support the health of the mother and the overall well-being of the puppies, including pre-breeding bloodwork and health assessments.

From ovulation timing to pregnancy monitoring via ultrasound imaging, we offer the clinical expertise needed to manage reproductive cycles effectively.

#### Our Breeding Services Include

  * Reproductive health evaluations to assess breeding readiness and overall health.
  * Breeding consultations and planning to support responsible breeding practices.
  * Pregnancy monitoring and guidance throughout gestation.
  * Pre-breeding health assessments to identify potential concerns before breeding.
  * Post-birth health support for both the mother and newborn puppies.

Detailed care during a breeding cycle ensures the health of the mother and promotes healthy developments for future litters. For those not planning to breed, learn about our spay & neuter services.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### What kind of dog breeding services do you offer?

We offer breeding-related veterinary services designed to monitor reproductive health. Our services include pre-breeding health evaluations, reproductive consultations, ovulation timing support (progesterone tracking), and pregnancy monitoring.

### Why is a pre-breeding health assessment so important?

A pre-breeding health assessment helps identify underlying health concerns early. Recognizing these factors before breeding can help support the health of the mother during the pregnancy cycle and contribute to the well-being of the future litter.

### How exactly do you watch a dog’s pregnancy?

We utilize clinical examinations and veterinary ultrasound imaging to confirm pregnancy, monitor gestational timelines, and track the development and health of the puppies.

### Can you help first-time dog breeders?

Yes. We offer professional consultations to help guide first-time breeders through the reproductive process, helping you understand veterinary timelines and care requirements before, during, and after a pregnancy. 

### Do you provide care once the puppies are born?

Yes, we offer postpartum health assessments for both the mother and her puppies to monitor nursing, growth, and general health during the early stages of neonatal life (whelping care).
