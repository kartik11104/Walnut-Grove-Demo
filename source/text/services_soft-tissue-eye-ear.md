## SOFT TISSUE, EYE & EAR PROCEDURES

Beyond routine surgery, our team performs various soft tissue procedures to address masses, injuries, and conditions affecting the eyes, ears, and internal organs.

Our surgical team prioritizes safety, precision, and compassionate care throughout every stage of treatment, using tailored anesthesia protocols for each patient. For ear and skin conditions that don't require surgery, see our skin & ear infections services.

#### Key Surgical Services Include

  * Soft tissue surgical procedures to treat internal medical conditions and remove abnormal growths.
  * Tumor and mass removal surgeries aimed at improving comfort and preventing complications.
  * Internal surgical treatments addressing digestive, reproductive, or structural concerns.
  * Pre-surgical consultations and planning to ensure every procedure is carefully evaluated.
  * Post-operative monitoring and recovery care supporting safe healing and patient comfort.

We utilize modern surgical techniques and monitoring equipment to support successful outcomes. Pre-surgical bloodwork is recommended before any procedure.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### What types of soft tissue procedures do you perform?

Our surgical care includes a variety of soft tissue procedures for dogs and cats, such as skin tumor and mass removals, corrective procedures for eye or ear tissue conditions, and surgeries addressing internal abdominal systems.

### How do I understand if my dog or cat needs a soft tissue procedure?

If your pet exhibits abnormal tissue growths, persistent discomfort, traumatic injuries, or chronic conditions affecting their eyes, ears, or internal organs, a thorough clinical examination will help determine if a surgical procedure is appropriate.

### Is anesthesia used during these procedures?

Yes. All soft tissue surgeries are conducted under monitored general anesthesia, utilizing individualized medication protocols to support a safe and stable surgical experience.

### What type of care does my pet receive after surgery?

Following their procedure, your pet is monitored in our recovery area until they are stable and awake. Upon discharge, we provide complete post-operative care instructions covering activity restrictions, incision monitoring, and precise pain medication schedules.

### What type of care does my pet receive after surgery?

We provide post-operative monitoring and recovery support to help your pet heal comfortably and safely following their procedure.
