## SPAY & NEUTER

Spaying and neutering are important preventive procedures that support your pet's long-term health and well-being. Beyond preventing unplanned litters, these surgical procedures play a key role in reducing the risk of serious health conditions and improving quality of life.

Our experienced veterinary team provides professional, compassionate spay and neuter care, using safe anesthesia protocols and guiding you through every step of the process. Pre-surgical bloodwork ensures your pet is ready for the procedure.

#### Benefits of Spaying & Neutering

  * Lower Risk of Hormone-Driven Aggression: Supports more stable behavioral patterns.
  * Prevention of Unplanned Litters: Responsible population control and community health.
  * Reduced Risk of Mammary Tumors: Significantly lowers cancer risk when performed early.
  * Reduced Prostate Disease Risk: Lowers incidence of benign prostatic enlargement and infections.
  * Long-Term Health & Longevity Benefits: Contributes to a healthier, more predictable life course.

We recommend discussing the ideal timing for your pet's procedure with your veterinarian, as age, breed, and health factors can influence the optimal approach. This is especially important for puppies and kittens.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### Why should I spay or neuter my dog or cat?

Spaying and neutering are common procedures that help prevent unplanned litters. These surgeries also reduce or eliminate the risk of certain serious health conditions later in life, such as uterine infections (pyometra) or reproductive tissue diseases.

### What is the best age to spay or neuter a dog or cat?

The optimal timing for a spay or neuter procedure can vary based on your pet’s breed, adult size, and overall development. Your veterinarian will work with you to determine the best timeline for your specific companion.

### Is spay or neuter surgery safe for dogs and cats?

While any medical procedure involving general anesthesia carries an inherent element of risk, spay and neuter procedures are routine surgeries performed under established safety standards. We perform a pre-surgical health evaluation, recommend bloodwork, and monitor vital signs to manage risks.

### Will spaying or neutering change my pet's behavior?

These surgeries can reduce behaviors driven by reproductive hormones, such as roaming, territorial urine marking, or certain hormone-related aggressive tendencies. However, your pet's core personality, affection levels, and intelligence remain the same.

### How long does it take for a pet to recover from the surgery?

Most dogs and cats heal smoothly within 10 to 14 days. Adhering closely to your veterinarian’s post-operative care instructions, including activity restriction and recovery cone use, will help support an uneventful recovery phase.
