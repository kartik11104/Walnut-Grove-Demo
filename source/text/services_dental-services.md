## PET DENTAL CARE

Oral health is deepy connected to your pet's overall wellbeing. Left untreated, dental disease can cause pain and lead to systemic issues affecting the heart, liver, and kidneys. As part of a comprehensive preventative care approach, we offer professional dental care to keep your pet's smile healthy and their breath fresh.

Our dental services include thorough cleanings, examinations, and polishing to remove plaque and tartar buildup. Advanced digital X-ray imaging helps us identify issues below the gumline.

#### Our Comprehensive Dental Services Include

  * Professional teeth cleaning and polishing
  * Dental X-rays for accurate diagnosis
  * Tooth extractions and oral surgical procedures when needed

#### Common Dental Problems

  * Periodontal (gum) disease and plaque buildup
  * Tooth decay and fractured or broken teeth
  * Oral infections, inflammation, and tumors
  * Persistent bad breath (halitosis)

#### Preventative Care

  * Regularly brushing your pet's teeth can effectively reduce the accumulation of harmful bacteria and plaque within its oral cavity.
  * It is generally more advantageous to adopt a proactive approach in contrast to a reactive one.
  * Human toothpaste should be avoided, as this can contain xylitol (also known as birch sugar), which is toxic to house pets.

Regular dental checkups and professional cleanings are key to preventing painful oral conditions and supporting long-term health. Many dental procedures require safe anesthesia to ensure your pet remains comfortable throughout.
