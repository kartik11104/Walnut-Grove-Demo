## SKIN & EAR INFECTIONS

Skin and ear conditions are among the most common reasons pets visit the veterinarian. Our team provides thorough examinations and diagnostic testing to determine the underlying cause of irritation, infection, or discomfort.

By identifying the source of the problem, we can provide targeted treatment and long-term management strategies. Persistent or severe cases may require soft tissue procedures for effective resolution.

#### Key Dermatology Services Include

  * Diagnosis and treatment of bacterial and yeast infections affecting the skin and ears.
  * Evaluation and management of allergy-related skin conditions that cause itching and inflammation.
  * Comprehensive ear examinations to identify infections, inflammation, or structural issues.
  * Skin cytology and diagnostic testing to accurately determine the cause of skin problems.
  * Long-term treatment plans designed to prevent recurring infections and maintain skin health.

Early diagnosis and proper treatment help restore comfort while preventing chronic skin and ear conditions. Bloodwork may be recommended to rule out underlying systemic causes.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### What are the most common symptoms that my dog or cat has some type of skin or ear problem?

Dogs and cats experiencing skin or ear discomfort frequently show signs such as regular scratching, head shaking, rubbing their face or body against surfaces, or displaying red, inflamed, or irritated skin.

### How do you figure out what is causing my pet’s skin or ear infection?

We perform a comprehensive physical examination and may recommend diagnostic tests such as skin or ear cytology. By evaluating these microscopic samples, we can determine whether the underlying cause involves bacteria, yeast, or environmental factors.

### Can skin and ear infections return after treatment?

Yes, infections can recur, particularly if they are linked to ongoing environmental or food allergies. For chronic cases, we establish long-term management plans to help support the skin barrier and reduce the frequency of future flare-ups.

### Why would my pet require bloodwork for a skin problem?

Chronic or recurring skin and ear problems are sometimes initiated by an underlying systemic health condition, such as endocrine or metabolic disorders. Bloodwork helps rule out these conditions so we can address the root cause rather than only managing visible signs.

### What happens if my pet’s ear or skin condition is actually severe?

Most skin and ear conditions are managed effectively with targeted medical therapies. However, if the condition becomes chronic, severe, or causes structural changes to the tissues that do not respond to standard treatments, soft tissue surgical procedures may be recommended to help manage chronic symptoms and support comfort.
