## MICROCHIPPING

Microchipping provides permanent identification that significantly increases the chances of reuniting lost pets with their families. The procedure is quick, safe, and can be performed during a routine wellness visit or alongside spay & neuter surgery.

Each microchip contains a unique identification number that can be scanned by veterinary clinics, shelters, and animal control organizations.

#### Key Microchipping Services Include

  * Safe and minimally invasive microchip placement performed during a routine appointment.
  * Microchip registration guidance to ensure your pet's information is correctly stored in national databases.
  * Microchip scanning and verification to confirm proper chip placement and functionality.
  * Microchip placement during routine visits or surgical procedures for added convenience.
  * Lost pet identification support when a scanned microchip helps reunite pets with their families.

Microchipping offers a reliable layer of protection that can greatly improve the chances of bringing a lost pet back home. We recommend it for all puppies and kittens as part of their early care.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### Is microchipping a safe procedure for dogs and cats?

Yes, microchipping is a quick, safe, and minimally invasive procedure. The microchip is roughly the size of a grain of rice and is implanted quickly beneath the loose skin between the shoulder blades, causing minimal temporary discomfort.

### Where can I get pet microchipping in Langley, BC?

Walnut Grove Animal Hospital provides pet microchipping in Langley, BC for both dogs and cats, with implantation done in seconds during any wellness visit.

### What occurs after my pet receives a microchip?

Implanting the chip is only the first step; you must register the unique chip number with your current contact details in the national database. Our team will provide you with the registry information to ensure your account is successfully updated.

### Where can I microchip a dog near me?

Look for a full-service veterinary clinic offering same-day microchip implantation and registration. Walnut Grove Animal Hospital in Langley offers both, 7 days a week.

### Is there a cat microchip clinic in Langley?

Yes, Walnut Grove Animal Hospital in Langley microchips cats during routine visits or alongside spay/neuter surgery, using a quick, low-stress procedure.

### Can microchipping be done at the same time as spay/neuter surgery?

Yes, for convenience and pet comfort, a microchip can be placed during a regular preventative care exam or easily implanted while your pet is undergoing another surgical procedure, such as a routine spay or neuter.

### How does microchipping help with lost pet identification in Langley?

A microchip gives shelters, vets and animal control in Langley a scannable ID number linked to your contact details, greatly improving the odds of reuniting you with a lost pet.

### How do I complete pet microchip registration in BC?

After implantation, you register the chip's unique ID number and your contact info in a pet-recovery database and must update it whenever you move or change your phone number.

### Can a vet check that my pet's microchip is still working?

Yes, microchips can be scanned and verified during their annual wellness exams to verify that it is fully functional, reading accurately and remains properly positioned.

### How does a microchip actually help reunite lost pets with owners?

Shelters, clinics and animal control scan the chip for its ID number, then look up your contact details in a national pet-recovery registry.

### How much does pet microchipping cost in Langley, BC?

About $40–$75 CAD one-time for the chip and implantation, plus a separate registry fee to store your contact details.
