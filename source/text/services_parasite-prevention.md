## PARASITE PREVENTION

Parasites can affect pets throughout the year and may lead to significant health problems if left untreated. Our parasite prevention programs are designed to protect pets from both internal and external parasites, and form a key part of our preventative care approach.

Through regular screenings and preventative treatments, we help reduce the risk of infection and protect your pet's overall wellbeing. Routine bloodwork can help detect heartworm and other parasitic infections.

#### Key Parasite Prevention Services Include

  * Flea and tick prevention programs designed to provide year-round protection against external parasites.
  * Heart-worm testing and preventative treatment plans to protect dogs and cats from serious cardiovascular disease.
  * Routine intestinal parasite screening to detect common internal parasites early.
  * Preventative medication recommendations tailored to your pet's lifestyle and exposure risk.
  * Seasonal parasite risk assessments to ensure your pet remains protected throughout the year.

Consistent parasite prevention helps safeguard not only your pet's health but also the health of your household. It is especially critical for puppies and kittens during their early development.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### Why is parasite prevention important for dogs and cats?

Parasite prevention helps protect your companion from external vectors like fleas and ticks, as well as internal threats like heartworms and intestinal parasites. Unmanaged infestations can lead to serious health conditions, and some parasites are zoonotic, meaning they can pose risks to human household members.

### How do you check if my pet has parasites?

We utilize regular screening protocols, including physical exams, microscopic fecal analyses, and specialized blood tests to detect the presence of parasites and identify infections at an early stage.

### How should my dog or cat usually receive parasite prevention treatments?

Many pets benefit from year-round, continuous preventative coverage. The specific medication and dosing schedule depend on your pet's individual lifestyle, daily environment, and geographical risk factors.

### What types of parasites do you help protect against?

Our preventative programs provide a line of defense against common external pests, including fleas and ticks, as well as dangerous internal parasites like heartworms, hookworms, roundworms, and tapeworms.

### Are parasite prevention plans customized for each pet?

Yes. We tailor our parasite prevention recommendations based on your pet’s age, weight, health history, and specific lifestyle exposure risks, matching the treatment to their individual needs.
