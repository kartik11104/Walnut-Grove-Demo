## PUPPY & KITTEN CARE

The early stages of life are essential for building a strong foundation of lifelong health. Our puppy and kitten care programs focus on growth monitoring, preventative medicine, and early behavioral guidance, including timely vaccinations and parasite prevention.

Through structured wellness visits and education for new pet owners, we help ensure young pets develop into healthy adult companions.

#### Key Puppy & Kitten Services Include

  * Early-life wellness examinations to monitor growth, development, and overall health.
  * Vaccination and deworming schedules carefully designed for young pets.
  * Development and growth monitoring to track physical and behavioural milestones.
  * Nutritional guidance to support healthy development during the early stages of life.
  * Preventative health education for owners to build confidence in caring for a new pet.

As your young pet grows, we also recommend discussing spay and neuter options and microchipping to ensure their long-term safety and health.

## Answers to the Most Common   
Questions that pet parent’s ask us!

### Where can I find a puppy and kitten vet in Langley?

Walnut Grove Animal Hospital in Langley offers dedicated puppy and kitten care, including first exams, vaccine series and deworming for newly adopted pets.

### Do you provide advice for first-time pet owners?

Yes. Walnut Grove Animal Hospital in Langley offers foundational puppy care and kitten care education, providing guidance on essential topics such as socialization, nutrition, basic training and establishing early preventative health routines.

### What is included in a puppy or kitten's first wellness exam in Langley, BC?

A pediatric wellness exam in Langley, BC includes a thorough physical evaluation, monitoring of growth and skeletal development, a dental assessment, congenital condition screenings and a review of initial vaccine requirements.

### What long-term health topics should I consult for my young pet?

During early veterinary visits, it is highly beneficial to discuss future milestones, including permanent identification via microchipping, establishing a life-stage vaccination timeline and planning for an optimal spay or neuter schedule.

### When should my kitten get its first vaccines in Langley?

Kittens typically start their first vaccines around 6–8 weeks old, with FVRCP and other core vaccines boosted every 3–4 weeks.

### How do I set up a puppy wellness plan near me?

A puppy wellness plan bundles wellness exams, vaccines, deworming, and parasite prevention into one age-based schedule — Walnut Grove Animal Hospital in Langley can build one at your first visit.

### What should new pet owners look for in a vet clinic in Langley?

Look for a clinic offering timely new-patient appointments, a clear puppy/kitten vaccine schedule and guidance on nutrition and spay/neuter timing.

### When is the right age to spay or neuter my puppy or kitten?

Usually 5–6 months for cats and small/medium dogs; larger breed dogs may wait longer to support joint development.

### What should I feed a growing puppy or kitten?

A food labeled "for growth" or "all life stages," fed in small portions several times a day, with gradual transitions to avoid digestive upset.

### What deworming schedule do puppies and kittens follow?

Deworming typically starts at 2–3 weeks old and repeats every 2–3 weeks through the vaccine series, since intestinal parasites are common in young pets.

### How much does a new puppy or kitten checkup cost in Langley?

First-year care covering exams, vaccines, and deworming typically runs $400–$900 CAD — ask Walnut Grove Animal Hospital for a Langley estimate.

### How many puppy/kitten wellness visits are needed before they are fully vaccinated? 

About 3–4 visits between 6 and 16 weeks of age, spaced roughly 3–4 weeks apart.
