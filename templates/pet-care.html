Halloween safety

Chocolate, especially dark and baking chocolate, and anything sweetened with xylitol can poison dogs. Keep the candy bowl out of reach, keep pets inside and away from the door on the night, and make sure costumes don't limit breathing or movement.

Pet food recalls

The Veterinary Information Network (VIN) keeps pet food and product recall information up to date. If you are worried about your pet's food or symptoms after eating it, call (604) 888-2628.
