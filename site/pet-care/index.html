Signs that need care right away:

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Collapse or seizure
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, rat bait, medications)
  • A swollen, hard belly with retching in dogs
  • A male cat straining to pee
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Hit by a car

Plants that are poisonous to pets

Plants add beauty to our homes and gardens, but some common varieties can be harmful to pets if chewed or swallowed. Cats and dogs explore with their noses and mouths. Know which plants may pose a risk.

    If your pet ate something on this list, call us right away.

    Halloween safety

    Chocolate, especially dark and baking chocolate, and anything sweetened with xylitol can poison dogs. Keep the candy bowl out of reach, keep pets inside and away from the door on the night, and make sure costumes don't limit breathing or movement.

    Pet food recalls

    The Veterinary Information Network (VIN) keeps pet food and product recall information up to date. If you are worried about your pet's food or symptoms after eating it, call (604) 888-2628.
