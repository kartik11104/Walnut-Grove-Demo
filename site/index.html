Meet Dr. Garg

Dr. Rattan Garg has worked in veterinary medicine since 1983, including years with the Canadian Food Inspection Agency and Metrotown Animal Hospital. He has looked after Walnut Grove's pets since 2007. His special interests are surgery and dog and cat medicine. At home there are two cats, Cinder and Emma, and a dog named Rocky.

Is this an emergency?

During our opening hours, we take emergencies. Call (604) 888-2628 and tell us you're on the way.

Call (604) 888-2628

When we're closed, go to the Animal Emergency Clinic of the Fraser Valley, open 24 hours: 6325 204th Street, Unit 302, Langley. (604) 514-1711.

Call (604) 514-1711Directions

Signs that need care right away:

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Collapse or seizure
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, rat bait, medications)
  • A swollen, hard belly with retching in dogs
  • A male cat straining to pee
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Hit by a car
