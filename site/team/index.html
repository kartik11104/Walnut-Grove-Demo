We're a small clinic on purpose. You'll see familiar faces at every visit.

Dr. Rattan Garg

Dr. Garg has worked in veterinary medicine and animal husbandry since 1983. He worked with Nestle Ltd, Metrotown Animal Hospital and the Canadian Food Inspection Agency before joining Walnut Grove Animal Hospital in 2007. His special interests are surgery and dog and cat medicine. He continues his education and enjoys music, running, camping and time with his family.

Member of the Canadian and BC Veterinary Medical Associations. Registered with the College of Veterinarians of British Columbia.

Is this an emergency?

During our opening hours, we take emergencies. Call (604) 888-2628 and tell us you're on the way.

Call (604) 888-2628

When we're closed, go to the Animal Emergency Clinic of the Fraser Valley, open 24 hours: 6325 204th Street, Unit 302, Langley. (604) 514-1711.

Call (604) 514-1711Directions

Signs that need care right away:

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Collapse or seizure
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, rat bait, medications)
  • A swollen, hard belly with retching in dogs
  • A male cat straining to pee
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Hit by a car
