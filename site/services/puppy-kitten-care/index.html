Puppy and kitten care

Young pets need care as they grow. We help you plan vaccines, parasite prevention, food and early health checks for your puppy or kitten.

What we do

At each visit, we check how your young pet is growing. We also talk with you about food, behaviour and care at home.

What's included

  • Exams to check growth and general health.
  • Vaccine and deworming schedules.
  • Checks on physical growth and behaviour.
  • Food advice for growing pets.
  • Help with questions about caring for a new pet.

As your pet grows, we can discuss microchipping and when to spay or neuter.

Questions

Puppies and kittens typically begin their initial vaccination series between 6 and 8 weeks of age, as maternal antibody protection begins to decrease. Boosters are then administered at regular intervals throughout their early growth to support development of proper immunity.

A pediatric wellness exam includes a full physical evaluation, monitoring of growth and skeletal development, a dental assessment, congenital condition screenings, and a review of initial health requirements.

We track your young pet's weight and behaviour at regular visits. This helps us check how they are growing.

Yes. We offer foundational puppy care and kitten care education, providing guidance on essential topics such as socialization, nutrition, basic training, and establishing early preventative health routines.

During early veterinary visits, it is highly beneficial to discuss future milestones, including permanent identification via microchipping, establishing a life-stage vaccination timeline, and planning for an suitable spay or neuter schedule.

Signs that need care right away:

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Collapse or seizure
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, rat bait, medications)
  • A swollen, hard belly with retching in dogs
  • A male cat straining to pee
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Hit by a car
