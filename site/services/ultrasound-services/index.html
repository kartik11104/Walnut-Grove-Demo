Ultrasound

Ultrasound uses sound waves to show organs and soft tissues as they move. It helps us investigate symptoms and monitor pregnancy or ongoing illness.

What we do

We look at live images of your pet's organs. Ultrasound can reveal changes that are hard to find during an exam or with blood tests alone.

What's included

  • Checks of the liver, kidneys, bladder and digestive tract.
  • Checks for lumps, fluid or changes in organs.
  • Pregnancy and reproductive health checks.
  • Investigation of pain, weight loss, vomiting or appetite changes.
  • Follow-up images for ongoing conditions.

We may use ultrasound alongside X-rays and blood tests.

Before your visit

If we are scanning your pet's abdomen, fasting (such as skipping breakfast) is generally recommended. A stomach containing food or excess gastrointestinal gas can obstruct the veterinary team's view of surrounding internal organs.
