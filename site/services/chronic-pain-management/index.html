Chronic pain

Chronic pain, often caused by arthritis or age-related conditions, can significantly impact your pet's quality of life.

What we do

Chronic pain, often caused by arthritis or age-related conditions, can significantly impact your pet's quality of life. We focus on identifying the source of discomfort through diagnostic tools like bloodwork and X-ray imaging, and creating individualized multimodal plans to manage pain effectively.

Our goal is to improve mobility, increase comfort, and help your pet stay active and happy in their daily life. Regular wellness exams can help detect pain early.

Our Pain Management Approach Includes

  • Assessment and treatment of osteoarthritis and joint pain commonly seen in aging pets.
  • Long-term pain monitoring and evaluation to adjust treatment plans as your pet's condition evolves.
  • Medication and therapeutic treatment strategies designed to manage inflammation and discomfort.
  • Mobility support recommendations including lifestyle adjustments and supportive therapies.
  • Ongoing quality-of-life evaluations to ensure your pet remains comfortable and active.

Regular evaluations allow us to adjust protocols as needed making sure your pet receives optimal relief.

Questions

Slowing down, experiencing stiffness after resting, or hesitating on stairs are often signs of chronic pain rather than just normal aging. A thorough veterinary assessment can help determine your dog's comfort levels and guide appropriate management strategies.

Cats naturally hide pain very well. Instead of vocalizing, chronic pain in felines often manifests as subtle behavioural changes, such as hiding, a decreased willingness to jump onto furniture, hesitating before leaps, or changes in grooming habits.

Follow-up visits are typically scheduled more frequently at the beginning of a plan, such as every few weeks, to monitor your pet's response to treatment. Once comfort levels are stably managed, the intervals between evaluations are usually extended to every few months.

Yes, modifying your home environment can significantly support your dog or cat's quality of life. Providing supportive orthopaedic bedding, placing non-slip rugs on slick flooring, using ramps instead of steps, and helping them maintain a healthy body weight can help minimize daily joint strain.

We take pain management seriously and utilize multi-modal protocols. Your pet receives pain relief medications before the procedure begins, continuous general anesthesia during surgery, and tailored post-operative medications to help support a comfortable recovery at home.

Prior to the procedure, we will administer gentle sedation or pain relief to help ensure your pet is deeply relaxed, comfortable, and resting peacefully.
