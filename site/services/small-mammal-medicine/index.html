Small mammal medicine

Rabbits and pocket pets have their own health needs. We offer exams, food advice, dental care and treatment suited to small mammals.

What we do

Small mammals can hide signs of illness. We check their health and talk with you about changes in eating, movement or behaviour. Bloodwork can help find hidden problems.

What's included

  • Exams for rabbits, guinea pigs and other small mammals.
  • Advice on food and living space.
  • Dental and digestive health checks.
  • Tests and treatment for illness or behaviour changes.
  • Prevention advice for your pet's species.

Some small mammals have teeth that keep growing. Their dental care needs regular attention.

Related services

  • Skin and ear problems
  • Chronic pain
  • Dog breeding

Signs that need care right away:

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Collapse or seizure
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, rat bait, medications)
  • A swollen, hard belly with retching in dogs
  • A male cat straining to pee
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Hit by a car
