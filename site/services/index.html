Everyday care

Wellness exams

Check-ups, food advice and prevention plans for each stage of your pet's life.

Vaccines

Vaccines chosen for your pet's age, health and risk of infection.

Parasite prevention

Testing and prevention for fleas, ticks, heartworm and intestinal worms.

Puppy and kitten care

Growth checks, vaccines, deworming and practical advice for your new puppy or kitten.

Microchipping

A lasting ID number that helps clinics and shelters reunite lost pets with their families.

Finding answers

Bloodwork

Blood tests to check organ health, look for illness and guide care before surgery.

Lab services

In-house blood, urine and stool tests to help find the cause of illness.

Digital X-ray

Digital images of bones, joints and organs to help assess injuries and illness.

Ultrasound

Sound-wave images to check organs, investigate symptoms and monitor pregnancy.

Surgery and dental

Spay and neuter

Surgery to prevent unplanned litters, with advice on timing and recovery.

Pet surgery

Surgery for lumps and internal health problems, with planning and recovery care.

Soft tissue, eye and ear surgery

Surgical care for lumps, injuries and conditions affecting eyes, ears or internal organs.

Dental care

Teeth cleaning, dental X-rays and treatment for painful or damaged teeth.

Anesthesia

Anesthesia planned for your pet, with monitoring during the procedure and recovery.

Ongoing care

Skin and ear problems

Exams and tests for itching, ear pain, infections and recurring skin problems.

Chronic pain

Help with lasting pain, including arthritis, with regular checks and treatment adjustments.

Small mammal medicine

Health checks, food advice and dental care for rabbits and other small mammals.

Dog breeding

Health checks before breeding, pregnancy monitoring and care for mothers and newborn puppies.

Boarding

Cat boarding at the clinic, with daily feeding, litter care and veterinary supervision.

End-of-life care

Support with quality-of-life decisions, saying goodbye and aftercare arrangements.

  • A swollen, hard belly with retching in dogs
  • A male cat straining to pee
