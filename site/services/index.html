Everyday care

Wellness exams

Our preventative care programs are designed to protect your pet's health before problems arise.

Vaccines

Vaccinations play a critical role in protecting pets from infectious and potentially life-threatening diseases.

Parasite prevention

Parasites can affect pets throughout the year and may lead to significant health problems if left untreated.

Puppy and kitten care

The early stages of life are essential for building a strong foundation of lifelong health.

Microchipping

Microchipping provides permanent identification that significantly increases the chances of reuniting lost pets with their families.

Finding answers

Bloodwork

Blood testing provides important insights into your pet's internal health and organ function.

Lab services

Detailed lab testing is essential for accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

Digital X-ray

Digital X-ray imaging allows veterinarians to quickly evaluate bones, joints, and internal structures.

Ultrasound

Ultrasound imaging allows us to look inside your pet's body in a gentle, non-invasive way.

Surgery and dental

Spay and neuter

Spaying and neutering are important preventive procedures that support your pet's long-term health and well-being.

Pet surgery

Our surgical services address a wide range of medical conditions requiring precise intervention.

Soft tissue, eye and ear surgery

Beyond routine surgery, our team performs various soft tissue procedures to address masses, injuries, and conditions affecting the eyes, ears, and internal organs.

Dental care

Oral health is deeply connected to your pet's overall wellbeing.

Anesthesia

Safe anesthesia is an essential component of many veterinary procedures, including pet surgery, dental care, and spay & neuter procedures.

Ongoing care

Skin and ear problems

Skin and ear conditions are among the most common reasons pets visit the veterinarian.

Chronic pain

Chronic pain, often caused by arthritis or age-related conditions, can significantly impact your pet's quality of life.

Small mammal medicine

Small mammals such as rabbits and pocket pets require specialized medical knowledge and handling.

Dog breeding

We provide professional veterinary support for breeders, focused on canine reproductive health and successful litters.

Boarding

We offer cozy, individualized boarding specifically for our feline friends.

End-of-life care

Saying goodbye to a beloved pet is one of the most difficult decisions a pet owner will ever make.
