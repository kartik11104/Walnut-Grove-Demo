End-of-life care

Saying goodbye is hard. We can talk with you about your pet's comfort, help you weigh the choices and support your family through the next steps.

What we do

We listen to your concerns and help you assess pain and quality of life. Blood tests can help us understand your pet's current health. Pain management may help with comfort during difficult health problems.

What's included

  • Visits to discuss comfort, pain and quality of life.
  • A quiet, private space at the clinic.
  • An explanation of each step.
  • Choices about staying with your pet.
  • Private or communal cremation and memorial keepsakes.

Please call if you need to talk or ask questions. We can also help you find support for pet loss and grief.

Questions

Follow-up visits are typically scheduled more frequently at the beginning of a plan, such as every few weeks, to monitor your pet's response to treatment. Once comfort levels are stably managed, the intervals between evaluations are usually extended to every few months.

Our clinic provides a wide range of surgical care for dogs and cats. This includes routine elective procedures, such as spays and neuters, orthopaedic surgeries, as well as soft tissue surgeries. Soft tissue procedures may include the removal of lumps or masses, and surgeries addressing internal abdominal conditions.

We offer breeding-related veterinary services used to monitor reproductive health. Our services include pre-breeding health evaluations, reproductive consultations, ovulation timing support (progesterone tracking), and pregnancy monitoring.

Determining the right time to say goodbye is a deeply personal and difficult decision. We provide supportive quality of life consultations and diagnostic reviews to help you objectively assess your dog or cat's comfort and well-being, helping your family make an informed, compassionate choice.

We offer a quiet comfort space within our clinic. This area is specifically used to provide a peaceful, private, and low-stress environment for you and your pet.

Yes, you are welcome to remain with your pet throughout the procedure. We tailor the experience to suit what makes your family feel most supported and comfortable.

Before the procedure, we give gentle sedation or pain relief to help your pet rest comfortably.

We handle all aftercare arrangements with dignity and respect. You may choose between private cremation (where your pet's ashes are returned to you) or communal cremation, and we can assist you with memorial keepsakes.

The recommended frequency depends on your pet’s age, life stage, health status, and lifestyle. Your veterinarian will recommend a preventative health schedule tailored specifically to your pet's ongoing needs.

Formulated pediatric nutrition provides the specific caloric balance and nutrient ratios required to support rapid muscle development, bone growth, and cognitive function during the first year of life.

Our surgical care includes a variety of soft tissue procedures for dogs and cats, such as skin tumor and mass removals, corrective procedures for eye or ear tissue conditions, and surgeries addressing internal abdominal systems.

Following their procedure, your pet is monitored in our recovery area until they are stable and awake. Upon discharge, we provide complete post-operative care instructions covering activity restrictions, incision monitoring, and precise pain medication schedules.

We provide a full array of diagnostic testing, including: * *Bloodwork:* Evaluates red and white blood cells, platelets, glucose levels, electrolytes, and markers for kidney and liver function. * *Urinalysis and Fecal Testing:* Monitors urinary tract health, assesses renal filtration, and screens for internal gastrointestinal parasites. * *Screenings:* Evaluates for vector-borne diseases (such as tick-related illnesses), heartworm disease, and viral conditions like FeLV and FIV. * *Specialty Diagnostics:* Includes skin cytologies, ear swabs, and pancreatic biomarker tracking.

Our preventative programs provide a line of defense against common external pests, including fleas and ticks, as well as dangerous internal parasites like heartworms, hookworms, roundworms, and tapeworms.

Signs that need care right away:

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Collapse or seizure
  • Suspected poisoning (chocolate, xylitol, lilies for cats, rat bait, medications)
  • A swollen, hard belly with retching in dogs
  • A male cat straining to pee
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Hit by a car
