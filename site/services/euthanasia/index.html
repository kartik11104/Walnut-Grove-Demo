End-of-life care

Saying goodbye to a beloved pet is one of the most difficult decisions a pet owner will ever make.

What we do

Saying goodbye to a beloved pet is one of the most difficult decisions a pet owner will ever make. At Walnut Grove Animal Hospital, we are dedicated to supporting you with the utmost compassion, respect, and dignity during this tender time. Our team is here to help you evaluate your pet's quality of life and guide you through a gentle, peaceful transition when the time comes.

We believe that every pet deserves a peaceful, painless, and dignified farewell. Our chronic pain management services can help improve comfort during difficult health challenges, and our staff handles every detail with gentle care, focusing entirely on your pet's comfort and your family's emotional needs.

Our Supportive Services Include

  • Quality of life consultations to help you assess your pet's comfort, pain levels, and overall well-being.
  • A dedicated, quiet comfort space designed to provide a peaceful, stress-free setting for you and your pet.
  • Step-by-step guidance on what to expect, ensuring you feel supported and informed throughout the process.
  • Personalized options for family presence, allowing you to choose how you wish to say goodbye.
  • Compassionate post-care arrangements, including private or communal cremation services and memorial keepsakes.

We are here to support you at every stage of the decision-making process. Diagnostic tools like bloodwork can help assess your pet's current condition. Please do not hesitate to reach out if you need to talk, ask questions about the procedure, or receive resources on pet loss and grieving.

Follow-up visits are typically scheduled more frequently at the beginning of a plan, such as every few weeks, to monitor your pet's response to treatment. Once comfort levels are stably managed, the intervals between evaluations are usually extended to every few months.

Our clinic provides a wide range of surgical care for dogs and cats. This includes routine elective procedures, such as spays and neuters, orthopaedic surgeries, as well as soft tissue surgeries. Soft tissue procedures may include the removal of lumps or masses, and surgeries addressing internal abdominal conditions.

We offer breeding-related veterinary services designed to monitor reproductive health. Our services include pre-breeding health evaluations, reproductive consultations, ovulation timing support (progesterone tracking), and pregnancy monitoring.

We offer a quiet comfort space within our clinic. This area is specifically designed to provide a peaceful, private, and low-stress environment for you and your pet.

The recommended frequency depends on your pet’s age, life stage, health status, and lifestyle. Your veterinarian will recommend a preventative health schedule tailored specifically to your pet's ongoing needs.

Formulated pediatric nutrition provides the specific caloric balance and nutrient ratios required to support rapid muscle development, bone growth, and cognitive function during the first year of life.

Our surgical care includes a variety of soft tissue procedures for dogs and cats, such as skin tumor and mass removals, corrective procedures for eye or ear tissue conditions, and surgeries addressing internal abdominal systems.

Following their procedure, your pet is monitored in our recovery area until they are stable and awake. Upon discharge, we provide complete post-operative care instructions covering activity restrictions, incision monitoring, and precise pain medication schedules.

We provide a thorough array of diagnostic testing, including: * *Bloodwork:* Evaluates red and white blood cells, platelets, glucose levels, electrolytes, and markers for kidney and liver function. * *Urinalysis and Fecal Testing:* Monitors urinary tract health, assesses renal filtration, and screens for internal gastrointestinal parasites. * *Screenings:* Evaluates for vector-borne diseases (such as tick-related illnesses), heartworm disease, and viral conditions like FeLV and FIV. * *Specialty Diagnostics:* Includes skin cytologies, ear swabs, and pancreatic biomarker tracking.

Our preventative programs provide a line of defense against common external pests, including fleas and ticks, as well as dangerous internal parasites like heartworms, hookworms, roundworms, and tapeworms.
